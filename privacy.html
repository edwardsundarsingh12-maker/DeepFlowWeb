Overview

DeepFlow is a focus timer and focus tracker for Android. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is stored and why. DeepFlow works fully without an account, and nothing about what you do in other apps is ever recorded.

Data that stays on your phone

To time and track your focus, DeepFlow stores on your phone:

  • your focus sessions: task name, category, planned and actual length, date and time, whether it was completed, and any mood, note or favourite you add,
  • custom categories, goals, XP, levels and achievements,
  • your settings, theme, timer style and focus background, including a photo you pick as the background (kept in the app's private storage).

This data lives in the app's private storage. If you don't sign in, it never leaves your phone unless you export it yourself.

Data handled by other services

  • Optional Google sign-in (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore). If you choose “Continue with Google”, your account ID, email address and display name are used to sign you in. Your focus sessions (including mood, notes and favourites) and custom categories are backed up to a private space in Cloud Firestore that only your account can read, so they can sync to your other phones. Your Pro plan and a record of each Play purchase are also stored with your account. Goals, themes and settings are not uploaded.
  • Firebase Analytics. The app includes Google's Firebase Analytics, which may collect app-usage events, device and app information, an app-instance ID and approximate location derived from your IP address, to help us understand how the app is used and keep it stable. It does not receive your task names or notes.
  • Google AdMob (free version only). A rewarded ad before a session and a banner on Home are provided by Google. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID, IP address and basic device information to show and measure ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. DeepFlow Pro removes ads completely.
  • Google Play Billing. DeepFlow Pro purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details; we receive a purchase token and order ID to confirm the purchase.

See Google's Privacy Policy for how Google handles this data. We don't sell your data or build advertising profiles.

Permissions and why they're used

  • Notifications: the live timer notification and “session complete” alerts.
  • Foreground service: keeps the timer running while DeepFlow is in the background.
  • Display over other apps (optional): Focus Lock, which shows your timer over other apps during a session. It is requested only when you tap the lock, and it is never used to read what is on your screen.
  • Vibration: start, finish and celebration haptics, each of which you can turn off.
  • Internet and network state: ads, Google Play purchases, analytics and optional sign-in and sync.
  • Billing: DeepFlow Pro purchases.

Your own photo for the focus background is picked through Android's system photo picker, so DeepFlow does not need storage permission.

Sharing

We don't share your data with anyone except the Google services listed above, which process it to provide sign-in, sync, analytics, ads and purchases. When you use the Share button after a session, you decide what is sent and to whom.

Retention and deletion

Data on your phone stays until you delete sessions in the app, clear the app's data or uninstall. Signing out never deletes anything on your phone. To delete your cloud backup and account data, email edwardsundarsingh12@gmail.com from the address you signed in with and we will remove it within 30 days.

Children

DeepFlow is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has signed in, contact us and we will delete the data.

Security

Data sent to Firebase is encrypted in transit, and access rules allow only your signed-in account to read or write your backup. No method of storage is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will also be noted in the release notes.

Contact
