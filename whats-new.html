What's New

Everything that has shipped in DeepFlow, newest first. Search to jump straight to a feature or a fix.

1.0.0 New 3 November 2026
  • First release. DeepFlow is here: a deep-work focus timer that keeps you in the work and shows your focus growing.
  • Focus timer. Quick starts of 25, 45, 60 and 90 minutes, any length from 5 to 240, nine categories plus your own, and a timer that keeps running in the background.
  • Focus Lock. Your timer covers other apps until you pause or stop, with press-and-hold unlocking.
  • Seven timer faces. Ring, Black hole, Bubbles, Flip paper, Orbit, Neon loader and Rolling.
  • Focus backgrounds. Eight animated scenes or your own photo, with dim and blur controls.
  • Deep Work Score. A daily 0–100 score from your focus time, streak and weekly consistency.
  • Progress. Totals, focus over time, time of day, categories and a consistency grid for any range.
  • History. Search, filter, favourite, and add a mood and notes to every session.
  • XP, levels and 11 achievements. With confetti, level-up and achievement celebrations.
  • Goals and widget. Daily to yearly focus goals, and a home-screen widget with today's focus and streak.
  • Themes. Light, dark, AMOLED, Material You, 8 accents and a custom colour wheel.
  • Backup. Optional Google sign-in with cloud sync across phones, plus file export and import.
